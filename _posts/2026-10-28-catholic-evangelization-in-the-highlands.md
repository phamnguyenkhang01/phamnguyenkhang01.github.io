title: Catholic Evangelization in the Highlands
---
Pastoral ministry in the highlands, where many people have yet to hear the Gospel, is still in need of the care and attention of the Catholic community.

It reminds me of the story of St. Thérèse. When Vietnam was still a relatively new land on the Catholic map, the call to go forth and proclaim Jesus Christ continued to echo throughout the Western Church. Missionaries made the long journey to the East to share Christ with others. The results were remarkable. Over the course of 400 years, Christianity grew tremendously in Vietnam.

I have read accounts of the Redemptorist missionaries who evangelized in Vietnam’s Central Highlands. The priests lived among the ethnic communities, simply sharing life with them, living and working alongside them. They had very little beyond a humble hut to call home. Yet through their presence, the grace of evangelization spread. The seeds they planted took root and blossomed, and many souls were brought to salvation.

What can we learn from these stories? God desires our cooperation. To cooperate with Him, we must learn to listen and encounter Him in prayer.

It is like the story of the miraculous catch of fish, when Jesus told St. Peter to cast his nets. Peter did as the Lord commanded, and he caught an enormous number of fish. Peter would also go on to bring many people to Christ, with 3,000 people responding to a single sermon. God continues to work through His Church today. He is still present with the faithful in the boats of their daily lives, calling them to cast their nets.

*Lord, keep us in Your boat. Help us to continue being Your fishers of men, according to the desire of Your Father's heart. Amen.*
